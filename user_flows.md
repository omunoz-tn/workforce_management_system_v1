# User Flows

## Supervisor Checking Daily Attendance
1. Supervisor logs in and lands on the Dashboard.
2. Views the "Late Arrivals" or "Offline" KPI cards.
3. Clicks on a KPI card to open the DrillDownModal.
4. Identifies employees who are late.
5. Expected Outcome: Supervisor contacts employees or adjusts the Schedule Board to reflect absence.

## Manager Analyzing Weekly Hours
1. Navigates to Analytics > Reports.
2. Selects "Employee Hours Report".
3. Uses the "Quick Select" dropdown to choose "Last Week".
4. Toggles "Adjusted Hours" to apply the 40-hour weekly cap logic.
5. Clicks "Export CSV".
6. Expected Outcome: Manager downloads a finalized hour report for payroll processing.

## Admin Managing Teams
1. Navigates to Employees > Teams.
2. Clicks "Add New Team".
3. Enters Team Name and selects Parent Group.
4. Uses the "Assign Members" modal to move employees from the "Unassigned" pool to the new team.
5. Expected Outcome: Employees are now mapped to the team for reporting and access control.
